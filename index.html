Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What curriculum does the school follow?+
We follow the Kenyan CBC (Competency-Based Curriculum) enriched with our Christian character development programme, additional academic enrichment in literacy and STEM, and the arts.
What is the school's Christian denomination?+
Applegate is non-denominational and welcomes families from all Christian backgrounds. Our foundation is based on core Biblical principles shared across denominations.
Are there bursaries available?+
Yes. We offer a limited number of partial bursaries for deserving families. Please contact our Bursar directly to enquire in full confidence.
Is there a waiting list for popular grades?+
Grade R, Grade 1, and Grade 7 fill fastest. We encourage early application. If unavailable, your child will be placed on our priority waiting list.
What are school operating hours?+
Monday–Friday, 7:30am–3:30pm. After-care available until 5:30pm (additional fee). Early drop-off from 7:00am for Grade 1 and above.

Our Journey

30 Years of Faithful Education

From a single classroom in 1994 to a thriving multi-phase campus — God has been faithful every step of the way.

1994

Applegate Founded

Founded with 12 students and a bold vision for Christ-centred education in Kakamega, Western Kenya.

1999

Primary School Opens

Growing demand led to full Primary School with Grades 1–6, a library, and dedicated classrooms.

2006

Junior School Added

Grades 7–9 introduced — completing the full Inception-to-Grade-9 journey.

2014

Campus Development

Science lab, sports field, and creative arts studio built to serve our growing community.

2025

350+ Students Strong

Today Applegate serves over 350 learners across Inception to Grade 9, with 42 dedicated educators on our Kakamega campus.
